# "Neon Echo" — Original Song

**Working title codename:** Neon Echo
**Genre:** Synthwave / Alt-Pop
**Tempo:** 108 BPM
**Key:** A minor (relative to C major)
**Time signature:** 4/4

## Concept
A song about signals that outlast the people who sent them — old messages, old
cities, old versions of yourself still transmitting in the dark. Built for a
widescreen, night-drive feel: pulsing arpeggios, a big open chorus, and a
bridge that drops to almost nothing before the last chorus rebuilds.

## Structure
Intro -> Verse 1 -> Pre-Chorus -> Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Pre-Chorus -> Chorus ->
Bridge -> Final Chorus (x2) -> Outro

## Chord chart (repeat per section)
- **Verse:** Am - F - C - G
- **Pre-Chorus:** F - G - Am - Am
- **Chorus:** C - G - Am - F
- **Bridge:** Dm - Am - F - G (half-time, sparse)

## Production notes
- Verse: gated pad + arpeggiated synth (16th notes), dry vocal, minimal drums (just kick + rim click).
- Pre-chorus: bring in a rising synth line + open hats to build tension.
- Chorus: full kit, layered vocal (doubled + octave-up harmony on "play it back"), wide stereo synth stabs on the downbeat.
- Bridge: strip to vocal + pad + a single analog delay throw, let the space breathe.
- Outro: fade the arpeggio out over 8 bars, end on a single sustained Am chord.
