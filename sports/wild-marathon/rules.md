# WILD MARATHON
### An original invented sport
**Working title codename:** Wild Marathon
**Category:** Team relay / precision hybrid
**Players:** 2 teams of 6 (4 active + 2 substitutes)
**Duration:** 4 quarters of 9 minutes ("legs"), with a 2-minute changeover
**Venue:** A hexagonal court, 30m across, with 6 raised "beacon" platforms
arranged around the perimeter and one central scoring ring.

## Concept
Wild Marathon combines relay-style continuous movement with precision
target-scoring. Teams never fully stop moving — the sport rewards
sustained pace *and* accuracy under fatigue, punishing teams who sprint
recklessly or play too cautiously.

## Equipment
- 1 "beacon ball" (a soft, palm-sized weighted ball that lights up briefly
  when it strikes a beacon platform).
- 6 beacon platforms, each with a pressure-sensitive scoring pad.
- 1 central ring (2m diameter) for bonus plays.
- Team sashes with embedded step-counters (for the Momentum rule, below).

## Objective
Score points by striking beacon platforms with the beacon ball while your
team maintains "Momentum" — a collective movement requirement that decays
if players stand still too long.

## Core rules
1. **Momentum.** Each active player's step-counter must register movement
   at least once every 4 seconds. If a player "stalls" for longer, their
   team's next beacon score is halved. This prevents pure stall-ball
   tactics.
2. **Scoring a beacon.** Striking a beacon platform with the ball scores 1
   point. Consecutive beacons struck without the opposing team touching
   the ball score an escalating bonus: 2nd = 2 pts, 3rd = 3 pts, capped at
   5 pts, then resets.
3. **The central ring.** If a team scores 3 beacons in a row (a "run"),
   they earn one uncontested Ring Play: 5 seconds of unopposed access to
   the central ring, where landing the ball inside scores a flat 8 points.
4. **Possession changes.** The ball changes possession if: it's caught out
   of bounds, a Momentum stall causes a scoring team to lose their next
   beacon attempt, or 20 seconds pass without any beacon being struck
   (a shot-clock style rule called "the Fade").
5. **Substitutions.** Unlimited substitutions, but only during a
   changeover or immediately after a Ring Play.
6. **Fouls.** Physical contact aimed at stopping movement (not the ball)
   is a "Drag" foul — awards the opposing team an automatic Ring Play.

## Winning
Highest total score after 4 legs wins. If tied, sudden-death: first team
to strike any 2 beacons in a row wins immediately.

## Why it's interesting to watch
- The Momentum rule keeps the game constantly in motion — no timeouts to
  "reset" strategy mid-possession.
- The escalating beacon combo + Ring Play creates comeback potential:
  teams can chain fast points late in a match.
- Small hexagonal footprint keeps it playable indoors, outdoors, in
  amateur leagues, or as a coached youth sport.

## Suggested first-season variants
- **Wild Marathon 3v3**: half court, 2 beacons removed, faster pace.
- **Wild Marathon Solo Trials**: single-athlete time trial version used for
  training and ranking ladders, scored purely on beacon accuracy per
  minute of sustained Momentum.
